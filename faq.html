Frequently asked questions

Everything you wanted to know about standing cardboard.

I did not sign up for this. Why do I have a membership?

Many of our members are enrolled by a thoughtful friend or family member. Congratulations, someone cares about you deeply. Log in with the email address they used to manage your membership.

How do I access my membership?

Use the Member Login button at the top of any page. You will need the email address your membership was registered under.

What if I forgot my password?

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page. If you still cannot get in, your enrolling friend or relative may know something you do not.

Can I choose which cutout I receive?

No. Every box is a surprise. Previous members have received dads, pirates, astronauts and one very large dog.

How big are the cutouts?

Most are between 5 and 6 feet tall. They ship flat and fold out with a built-in brace.

Can I cancel or pause my membership?

Of course. Log in to your account and choose Manage Membership. We will be sad, but our cutouts are not capable of crying.

Are the cutouts of real people?

Our cutouts are original characters. Any resemblance to a person living or dead is a coincidence and probably the cardboard's fault.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere a standard delivery truck can go. If it fits through your door, we will deliver it.
