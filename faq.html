Frequently asked questions

Everything you wanted to know about standing cardboard.

I did not sign up for this. Why do I have a membership?

Many of our members are enrolled by a thoughtful friend or family member. Congratulations, someone cares about you deeply. Log in with the username and temporary password from your order confirmation letter to manage your subscription.

How do I access my membership?

Use the Member Login button at the top of any page. You will need the username and temporary password printed on your order confirmation letter.

What if I forgot my password?

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page. If you still cannot get in, your enrolling friend or relative may know something you do not.

Can I choose which cutout I receive?

No. Every box is a surprise. Previous members have received dads, pirates, astronauts and one very large dog.

How big are the cutouts?

Every cutout is life-size, pre-cut on heavy-duty corrugated board with an attached fold-out easel back.

When will my first cutout ship?

Your first cutout enters production as soon as your order is placed and is dispatched within 3 to 5 business days via standard ground service.

When will I be billed?

Your initial payment is processed when your order is placed. After that you are billed monthly on the same date, so an order placed on the 25th renews on the 25th.

Can I cancel or pause my membership?

Of course. Log in to your account portal and choose Modify subscription or Cancel subscription before your renewal date. We will be sad, but our cutouts are not capable of crying.

Are the cutouts of real people?

Our cutouts are original characters. Any resemblance to a person living or dead is a coincidence and probably the cardboard's fault.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere a standard delivery truck can go. If it fits through your door, we will deliver it.
